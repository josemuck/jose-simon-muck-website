Leadership Narrative

Jose Simon Muck is a senior digital executive who has built products, led organizations, and driven transformation across Europe, Latin America, the Middle East, and the United States.

His career combines strategic and creative thinking with a deep ability to align people, translate complexity into direction, and turn cross-functional teams into high-performing execution engines. From leading a fintech company at CEO level to scaling a B2B SaaS forecast product used by 350,000 workers across 33 countries, Jose has consistently operated at the intersection of product, growth, and organizational leadership.

At ista, he leads the international digital strategy and governance for online platforms across 20+ markets — orchestrating a complex ecosystem of UX, SEO, content, analytics, and local market stakeholders into coherent, scalable execution.

Jose holds German and Peruvian nationality, is based in Germany, and brings a genuinely international lens to every challenge he takes on.
