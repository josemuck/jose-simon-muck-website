Leadership Narrative

Jose Simon Muck is a strategic operator and transformation leader who connects product, people, and execution across international organizations.

With 18+ years across startups, fintech, B2B SaaS, creative agencies, and multinational enterprise, his work sits at the intersection of product leadership, digital transformation, and organizational orchestration. From executive leadership within one of Peru's most significant fintech ecosystems, to scaling AI-powered SaaS platforms and directing international digital transformation across 27 markets — Jose has consistently built, scaled, and transformed digital operations in complex organizational environments.

Experienced leading across multilingual and multicultural environments in English, German and Spanish. With a German-Latin American background, Jose navigates different cultural expectations, communication styles, and leadership realities as a genuine operational advantage — not a footnote.

Currently orchestrating cross-market digital execution across Europe and the Middle East.

Beyond the Work

The work is shaped
by the person.

Leadership is ultimately a human endeavour. The perspective you bring to it is shaped by where you have lived, how you have communicated, what you have built, and who you have become in the process.

Jose is a multicultural leader who has spent his career navigating different cultural expectations, communication styles, and organizational realities — not as a challenge to manage, but as a genuine operational advantage. Growing up between German and Latin American influences, operating internationally in English, and speaking Spanish at home has shaped a leadership style that is naturally adaptive, contextually aware, and oriented toward building shared understanding across difference.

Beyond professional life, Jose is a husband and father of three, based in Düsseldorf, Germany. He is deeply interested in the intersection between technology, organizations, communication, and human behavior — and holds a strong conviction that storytelling, empathy, and strategic thinking are far more connected in modern leadership than most frameworks acknowledge.

He has also authored creative work, and his wife is a published author — a household where language, ideas, and narrative are taken seriously.
