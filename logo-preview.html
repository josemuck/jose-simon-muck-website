06 · Scale reference

Mark at all usage sizes

The calligraphic detail is preserved from 240px to ~40px. Below 32px the form simplifies into a readable silhouette. The favicon requires a separate simplified crop per the brand guide.

At 16–32px the full calligraphic mark compresses into a readable silhouette — identifiable but not detailed. The brand guide recommends a simplified crop for the favicon. On approval, a dedicated favicon.svg will be created using the upper JM region of the mark (the J flourish + M caps), cropped to a square with breathing room. This maintains brand coherence at browser-tab scale without forcing illegibility.
