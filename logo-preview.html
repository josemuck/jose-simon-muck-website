Five Concepts for the M Mark

Not calligraphy. Not signature. Each direction approaches the same problem differently: a personal mark that communicates "José Muck stands behind this." Five distinct philosophies — same palette, no changes to the site until you choose.

Abstract Chart / Mountain

Product-oriented. Geometric. Reads as startup or index fund. The five concepts below replace this with an M-based personal mark — each one a different answer to what "personal seal of quality" means.

1
The Constructed Mark
Precision · Slab terminals · Optical weight
Navigation
Footer
Jose Simon Muck
Rationale

An M built to a system. The outer stems carry more optical weight than the inner diagonals — a deliberate choice borrowed from premium type design, where strokes have a structural hierarchy rather than uniform weight. The horizontal slab caps at each stem end signal intentional construction: this letter was drawn, not typed. The valley is raised to 62% of the total height, the classical proportion of a refined uppercase M.

Symbolism: the mark communicates precision, durability, and craft. The same qualities you find on the title page of a book published by someone who cares about every detail. Works at any size without losing its character.

The JM Monogram
Initials fused · Hidden depth · Classic monogram
Navigation
Footer
Jose Simon Muck
Rationale

The J's descender and the M's left stem are the same line. Both initials occupy a single, unified mark. At a glance it reads as M — a clear, confident letterform. On second look, the J hook at the base reveals the full monogram. This is how luxury houses have built initials-based marks for over a century: two identities compressed into one shape.

Symbolism: hidden depth communicates that there is more to the person than what is immediately apparent. The mark is also fundamentally personal — it contains the specific initials of one individual. No other person could own this mark in the same way.

3
The Editorial Imprint
Ruled · Contained · Publisher's mark
Navigation
Footer
Jose Simon Muck
Rationale

The M is presented within a ruled frame — two precise horizontal lines that give the letter context and authority. The tick marks at each stem position are borrowed from technical drawing and measurement instruments: they signal that this mark was produced by a system, not improvised. The whole composition reads like a publisher's colophon, a chapter heading mark, or the imprint on the title page of a premium book.

Symbolism: the ruled container communicates structure, reliability, and discipline. The M is the author; the rules are the standards it adheres to. This mark says "editorial authority" more clearly than any other direction here. Note that its wider format makes it slightly different from the others — it's a horizontal mark, not a square one, which gives it a distinct character.

4
The Diamond Seal
Contained · Angular · Personal signet
Navigation
Footer
Jose Simon Muck
Rationale

The diamond is chosen over the circle deliberately. Circles suggest openness, inclusiveness, technology. Diamonds suggest precision, value, and directional intention. The contained mark has a long history in personal seals: signet rings, architect's stamps, engineer's marks, surveyor's reference points. The geometry here is exact: the M's valley sits at the precise center of the diamond — the two forms share a center point.

Symbolism: the diamond container communicates "I put my name on this." It is the visual equivalent of a seal on a document — the mark of authorship that gives weight to what follows. The angular container and the clean M inside create a tension between the geometric and the personal that feels contemporary without feeling like a startup.

5
The Block Mark
Weight · Conviction · Letterpress quality
Navigation
Footer
Jose Simon Muck
Rationale

The M rendered at maximum visual weight. Where the other four concepts use fine or medium strokes, this one commits fully to the letterform as a physical object — the kind of mark you find on a letterpress print, a linocut, or a metal type block. The flat end-caps and miter join at the valley give it the quality of something pressed into paper rather than drawn on it. The subtle impression layer simulates the slight ink spread of a real stamp.

Symbolism: weight equals conviction. This is the M of someone who has no hesitation about who they are. It is also the most immediately recognizable of the five — at favicon size, at large size, in print or screen, the mark holds its identity with no ambiguity. Not the most delicate choice, but possibly the most memorable.
